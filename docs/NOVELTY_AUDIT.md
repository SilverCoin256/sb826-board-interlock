## What has already been done vs. never tested

**Already done:** (a) whether SB826 changed board *composition/compliance counts* — saturated (Allen & Wahid 2024, *Management Science*, and others from Stage 1's gender-quotas-boards sweep); (b) whether interlock-network *position* predicts diffusion of a diversity attribute — done, closely, by Mateos de Cabo et al. (2024); (c) market reaction to the 2022 invalidation — done by Klick (pending detail verification); (d) decoupling with network centrality as predictor of a conventionally-measured decoupling outcome — done, in a different country/mandate, by the 2022 China CSR paper.

**Never tested, per two independent thorough sweeps:** network topology (density/centralization/elite-cohesion) as the outcome variable of a board-diversity legal mandate; decoupling operationalized through network structure rather than text/rating gaps; any network-structural analysis of the post-invalidation (2022-present) period for SB826, Nasdaq, or any other board-diversity mandate anywhere.

## Conservative verdict: MODERATE novelty

Not incremental — the topology-as-outcome + reversal-as-natural-experiment combination is genuinely absent from a well-searched literature, not just under-cited. Not substantial/field-opening — the surrounding space is populated enough (five closely-adjacent papers, one of them very close) that this reads as a well-targeted gap inside a live, active research conversation, not an undiscovered continent. The paper's contribution has to be earned through precise differentiation from Mateos de Cabo et al. (2024) specifically, stated explicitly in the introduction, not asserted as general novelty.
